About me

I'm Toluwani Abiodun, a front-end developer who builds responsive websites for businesses. I care about clear communication, thoughtful design and making sure every page works well on real screens.

A clear purpose behind every page

A website should help people understand a business and feel confident taking the next step.

I start by understanding who the site is for and what visitors need to do. Then I organise the content, create a visual direction and build a responsive experience that supports those goals.

I aim for websites that are easy to use, quick to load and straightforward to maintain after launch.

How I work

A considered process, from the first conversation through to launch.

Understand

I learn about your audience, your goals and the information your website needs to communicate.

Design

I shape a clear visual direction with useful hierarchy and readable content.

Build and refine

I turn the design into a responsive website, then test and refine it across screen sizes.
